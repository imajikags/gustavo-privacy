Додаток використовує Unity Engine та Unity IAP, які можуть обробляти технічну інформацію про пристрій (тип пристрою, версія ОС) для роботи покупок і діагностики. Дивіться політику конфіденційності Unity.

Діти

Privacy Policy — Gustavo Adventures

Last updated: 2026-10-15

Data we collect

We do not collect or share any personal data: no name, e-mail, location, contacts or advertising identifiers.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, crystal balance and settings (language, volume) are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play (Android), the App Store (iOS) or Steam. We do not receive or store your payment details. Their handling is governed by the respective platform's policies.

Third parties

The app uses Unity Engine and Unity IAP, which may process technical device information (device type, OS version) to enable purchases and diagnostics. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the current version is always available on this page.
